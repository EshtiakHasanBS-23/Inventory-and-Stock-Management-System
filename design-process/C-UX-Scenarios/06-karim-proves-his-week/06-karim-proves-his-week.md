# 06: Karim Proves His Week

**Project:** Mojud
**Updated:** 2026-11-16 (as built)
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)
**Priority:** P2
**Status:** ✅ Built

---

## Transaction (Q1)

Show his own work for today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days, as proof of what he did.

## Business Goal (Q2)

The record that holds people accountable also protects honest workers, which keeps the floor willing to record everything.

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** [Karim, Warehouse worker](../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/02-Karim-the-Warehouse-Worker.md); [Rafiq, Manager](../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/05-Rafiq-the-Manager.md) (looks at Karim's record)
**Situation:** Saturday. Rafiq asks why the rice stack is short. Karim wants to show what he recorded this week and that he flagged the wet bags himself.

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Proof with his name that protects him.
**Worry:** Being blamed for someone else's gap.

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Phone.
**Entry:** Menu (bottom tab bar) "My record".

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User success:** Karim shows "Last 7 days: Stock in and out 14 (86 in · 18 out); Damage flags 1 (1 approved · 0 rejected · 0 waiting)". Rafiq, on his own phone, picks "Karim" in "Person" and sees the same.
**Business success:** A dispute is settled from the record, without anyone editing anything.

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **My record (06.1):** Karim opens it; his name and three cards (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days) show. He hands the phone to Rafiq. ✓
2. Alternatively, Rafiq opens My record and picks Karim in "Person".
