## Overview

**Purpose:** A person's own work in three periods, as proof of what they did: stock in and out, counts with what they found, damage flags raised with their outcome, and decisions made for others.
**User situation:** Karim shows Rafiq what he recorded this week.
**Entry:** Menu "My record".
**Exit:** Stays.

## Layout

- **Desktop:** page header; Person picker (Owner, Manager); the person's name; three cards side by side: "Today", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days".
- **Phone:** the three cards stack, full width. Each card has four lines: icon, label with detail below, and the count on the right.

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading | "Loading…" |
| Load failed | "Couldn't load this. Refresh the page to try again." |
| No work yet | The three cards with zeros (no separate empty message) |
| Other person picked | Name and cards change to that person |

## Refusal Messages (API)

| Trigger | Message |
|---|---|
| A Supervisor, Worker, Sales rep or Accountant asks for someone else's record | "You can see only your own record." |

## Accessibility

- Each card is a `section` with its heading; every number sits beside its label and detail text, so the meaning does not depend on position.
- Icons are decorative.

## API

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/me/record` | Own record |
| `GET /api/v1/me/record?userId=<id>` | Owner, Manager picking a person |
| `GET /api/v1/users/names` | People for the picker |

Response: `{name, today, week, month}`, each period `{movements, unitsIn, unitsOut, counts, countMissing, countExtra, flagsRaised, flagsApproved, flagsRejected, flagsPending, decisions}`.
