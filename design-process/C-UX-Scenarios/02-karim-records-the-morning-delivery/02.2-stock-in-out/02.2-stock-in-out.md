## Overview

**Purpose:** Record one stock change: product, direction, reason, quantity and an optional note. The saved message shows the new balance.
**User situation:** Karim records 40 bags delivered, then 6 bags sold.
**Entry:** Menu "Stock in / out"; "Stock in / out" chip on a product row (product pre-chosen); "Stock in" link in the Dashboard's Needs restocking list (product pre-chosen).
**Exit:** Stays on the page for the next entry; "Flag damage" link → `/damage` (keeps the chosen product).

## Layout

- **Desktop:** heading; the "Found damaged stock? Flag damage" hint; saved message; a white panel with the form: Product, in-stock line, two large direction tiles side by side, then Reason and Quantity in a two-column grid, Note, Save.
- **Phone:** one column at full width; the tiles stay large touch targets; the bottom tab bar stays visible and the page has bottom padding so Save is never hidden behind it.

## Behaviour

- On success the quantity and note clear; product, direction and reason stay, ready for the next entry. Products, History, Dashboard, Report, Approvals and My record data refresh.
- Each save is one movement in the permanent record: product, direction, reason, quantity, balance after, unit cost at that moment, note, person and time. It can never be edited or deleted (see [DD-2](../../00-design-decisions.md)).
- The stock-out check and the update happen in one database transaction with a conditional update, so two people taking the last unit at the same moment cannot both succeed.

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|

| Product chosen | In-stock line |

| Saved | Saved message with the new balance |
| Refused | Message under the field the server named; values kept |
| No products in the business | "There are no products yet. Add products first." above the form |

## Validation and Refusal Messages (`packages/contracts/src/stock.ts`, API)

| Trigger | Message | Where |
|---|---|---|
| No product | "Choose a product." | Under Product |
| Reason does not fit direction | "Choose a reason that fits the direction." | Under Reason |
| Quantity empty, 0, decimal or above 1,000,000 | "Enter a whole number, 1 or more." | Under Quantity |
| Note over 200 characters | "Keep the note under 200 characters." | Under Note |
| Stock out larger than stock | "Only 52 bag in stock." | Under Quantity |
| "Damaged" sent by a role other than Owner or Manager | "Use Flag damage: a supervisor, manager or owner decides it." | Under Reason |
| Other failure | "Couldn't save. Try again." | Error summary |

## Accessibility

- The direction tiles are a `fieldset` with legend "Direction"; each tile is a labelled radio button, so arrow keys move between them.
- The saved message is announced (`role="status"`).
- Direction is shown by icon and word as well as colour.

## API

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/products` | Fill the product list and in-stock line |
| `POST /api/v1/stock/movements` `{productId, direction, reason, quantity, note?}` | Save (201 movement with `balanceAfter`); 409 `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`; 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` |
