## Overview

**Purpose:** One inbox for everything that needs a second person: damage flags and reversal requests. Each is decided in one tap.
**User situation:** Nasima clears the day's damage flags at the end of her shift. In scenario 05, Rafiq approves Haque's reversal here.
**Entry:** Menu "Approvals"; the Dashboard banner "N requests waiting for approval" (`pending-approvals`).
**Exit:** Stays on the page.

## Who may decide

| Kind | May decide | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Damage flag | Supervisor, Manager, Owner | The person who flagged it |
| Reversal | Manager, Owner | The person who asked for it |

This is checked in the API and by a database rule, not only by hiding buttons.

## Layout

- **Desktop:** page header with title and intro; two tab buttons; notice or error line; a list of cards.
- **Phone:** the same cards at full width; the two decide buttons sit side by side and wrap when narrow.

## What a decision does

| Kind | Approve | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Damage flag | Written off: the units leave the damaged hold; the loss stays | A "Flag rejected, back in stock" movement returns the units to sellable stock |
| Reversal | The opposite "Reversal" movement is recorded, linked to the original | Nothing changes; it may be asked again |

A reversal that would take stock below zero is refused ("Only N <unit> in stock.") and stays waiting.

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading | "Loading…" |
| Load failed | "Couldn't load this. Refresh the page to try again." |
| Nothing waiting | Check icon + "Nothing is waiting for a decision." (`approvals-empty`) |
| Nothing decided | Check icon + "Nothing has been decided yet." |
| Decided | Notice; the card leaves Waiting; all stock data refreshes |
| Refused | Error line; the card stays |

## Refusal Messages (API)

| Trigger | Message |
|---|---|
| Someone else decided first (row lock; only one decision wins) | "This was already decided." |
| Deciding your own request | "Someone else must decide your own request." |
| Worker, Sales rep or Accountant decides damage | "A supervisor, manager or owner decides damage." |
| Supervisor or below decides a reversal | "An owner or manager decides reversals." |
| Reversal would make stock negative | "Only N <unit> in stock." |

## Accessibility

- Tabs are a labelled group of toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`).
- Kind, outcome and amounts are words and icons, never colour alone.
- Notice uses `role="status"`; refusal uses `role="alert"`.

## API

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/approvals?status=pending` / `?status=decided` | Load a tab; each item carries `canDecide` |
| `POST /api/v1/approvals/:id/approve` | Approve |
| `POST /api/v1/approvals/:id/reject` | Reject |
