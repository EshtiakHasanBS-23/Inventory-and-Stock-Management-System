## Overview

**Purpose:** Report damaged or expired units. They leave sellable stock at once into a damaged hold, and a pending approval opens for a Supervisor, Manager or Owner other than the person who flagged.
**User situation:** Karim has two wet bags in front of him.
**Entry:** "Flag damage" link on Stock in / out (`stock-flag-link`, keeps the chosen product); "Flag damage" chip on a product row (product pre-chosen).
**Exit:** Stays on the page; menu "Approvals" to see the request.

## Layout

- **Desktop:** page header with title and intro; flagged message; a white panel with Product, in-stock line, Damaged quantity, Note and the red "Flag damage" button.
- **Phone:** the same panel at full width, one column.

## Behaviour

- Saving records a "Damage flagged" stock-out movement under the person's name and opens a pending damage approval for that quantity, at the product's current unit cost.
- The units now show on Products as "N held as damaged" and on the Dashboard banner "৳X held as damaged, waiting for a decision".
- After saving, quantity resets to 1 and the note clears; the product stays chosen.

## Accessibility

- The red button also carries an alert icon and the words "Flag damage", so its meaning does not rely on colour.
- The flagged message is announced (`role="status"`).
