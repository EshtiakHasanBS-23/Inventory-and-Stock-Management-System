## Overview

**Purpose:** The owner's view of stock and money: what stock is worth, what was lost in the last 30 days and why, what needs restocking, and what happened last. Every loss figure links to the movements behind it.
**User situation:** Haque checks the business from a distance and wants to know where ৳5,002 of losses came from.
**Entry:** After sign-in or sign-up; menu "Dashboard".
**Exit:** Banner → Approvals; loss rows → History filtered by reason; "Stock in" → Stock in / out with the product chosen.

## Layout

- **Desktop (1200 px and up):** page header; optional banner; a grid of tiles (two large hero tiles: Stock value, Losses; four small tiles: Low stock, Out of stock, Products, Units in stock); then two cards side by side: "Needs restocking" and "Recent movements".
- **Below 1200 px:** the two cards stack.
- **Phone:** tiles wrap to fit; cards full width; the bottom tab bar stays visible.

## Behaviour

- Figures count up from their previous value over 0.7 s when they load; when reduced motion is requested they show their final value at once.
- Data refreshes after any stock change made in this browser.

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading | Four grey skeleton blocks (`aria-busy`), visually hidden "Loading…" |
| Load failed | "Couldn't load this. Refresh the page to try again." |
| New business | Tiles at 0, no banner, "Every product is above its minimum stock.", "No stock movements yet." |
| Something waiting | Banner on top |

## Accessibility

- Tiles are a description list (`dl`): label in `dt`, figure in `dd`, so each number is read with its label.
- Low / Out tiles and pills carry icons and words; the losses figure is not conveyed by red alone.
- Cards are `section`s labelled by their headings.
