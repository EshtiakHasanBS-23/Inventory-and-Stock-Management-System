## Overview

**Purpose:** Add the people who work in the business, each with a role, and switch a person off (deactivate) or back on.
**User situation:** Haque adds Karim, Nasima and Rafiq so they can start recording tomorrow.
**Entry:** Menu "Staff".
**Exit:** Stays on the page; menu to anywhere.

## Layout

- **Desktop:** page header; "Add a staff member" panel with a four-column form grid (Name, Phone number, Password, Role); then "Your team" toolbar with count; then the team table.
- **Tablet (700–1199 px):** form in two columns.
- **Phone (below 700 px):** form in one column; each team member becomes a card: avatar and name as title, then Phone number, Role, Status with labels, then the action chips.

## Behaviour

- A deactivated person cannot sign in (they see "This account is deactivated." after a correct password), and any session they hold ends on its next request. Their past movements stay in the record.
- Reactivating lets them sign in again with their existing password.

## States

## Validation and Refusal Messages (`packages/contracts/src/staff.ts`, API)

| Trigger | Message | Where |

| Name empty | "Enter the person's name." | Under Name |
| Phone not 6–15 digits | "Enter a phone number of 6 to 15 digits." | Under Phone number |
| Phone already registered (any business) | "This phone number is already registered" | Under Phone number |
| Password 8–128 characters | "At least 8 characters." | Under Password |
| No role | "Choose a role." | Under Role |
| Try to deactivate the Owner (API) | "The owner cannot be deactivated." | Row error |
| A non-Owner calls the API | "Only the owner can manage staff." | – |

## Accessibility

- Row action chips carry the person's name in their accessible name, so a screen reader hears "Deactivate Karim", not just "Deactivate".
- Status pills combine icon and word.
- The avatar initial is `aria-hidden`.

## API

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/users` | Load the team |
| `POST /api/v1/users` `{name, phone, password, role}` | Add (201) |
| `PATCH /api/v1/users/:id` `{active}` | Deactivate / reactivate |
